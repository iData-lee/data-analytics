# Capstone - EmporiUm Student Bookstore (Maryland) 

## Project Overview 
EmpooriUm is a growing student bookstore chain that sells Technology products, Art Supplies and various other products; both in store and online. I am the new Data Analyst in the Maryland sales territory. My Sales Manager wants my analysis and instruction on the performance of Maryland sales team and within the [region] region. 
Using SQL in MySQL Workbench I will analyze the total revenue for sales in the MD Territory, monthly and yearly trends, a comparison to MD total revenue to the Regions total revenue, transactions and average sale amount by product category, and MD sales performance ranking either in store or online. 
In conclusion, I will provide my recommendations on where next quarters sales attention should be focused.
