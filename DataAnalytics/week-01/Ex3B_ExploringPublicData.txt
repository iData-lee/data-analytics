1. Start by reviewing the data.gov home page. What is this?

This site is the homebase for all US Government Open Data

2. Go to Most Viewed Datasets. How many datasets are listed?

On the Most Viewed Dataset Page there are 20 listings on the first page but a total of 590,238 datasets are available on Data.gov

3. Find the Topic Category for Human Health. Review the range of data sources
available for this topic. Why do you think these were tagged with the Human Health
category?

There is a wide range and variety of topics in the Human Health topic. A lot has to do with death tolls, Covid-19, Food Nutrition, baby names and behavioral health etc. All of which has to do with the overall health and wellbeing of human life and health.

4. Go to the Food Price Outlook. Review the catalog page for this dataset. How can you
navigate to additional datasets from the USDA?
 
To find related datasets there is a section of Tags that can be added for similar datasets.
